# Glossary #jevgate/glossary

- **Entry**: one debit or credit line against an account, always part of a balanced posting.
- **Posting**: a set of entries that sums to zero; the unit of every write.
- **Period**: a month of a tenant's books; closed periods are immutable.
- **Tenant**: one customer's books; every row carries its tenant id.
- **Minor units**: integer cents (or equivalent); Ledger never stores a float.
